# BrasserieDuFjord-Seasonal-Launch-Plan.pptx — expected elicitation

Genre: marketing launch plan for a seasonal beer. Craft identity: kraft-paper picture background, serif
italics, badges (stars, ribbon, 24-point seal), freeform curves. 12 slides.

## Slide 2 — The beer (radar chart, tasting card)
Marée d'Automne vs Fjord Pale Ale on Malt, Hops, Bitterness, Sweetness, Body, Aroma. Specs: amber ale,
6.2 % ABV, 28 IBU, 32 EBC, chestnuts.

## Slide 3 — Personas (round-cropped photos, ellipse callouts, one group per persona)
Concept `Persona`: Gaëlle, 34, graphic designer (Brest); Marc, 52, crêperie owner (Quimper); Léo, 27,
nurse (Rennes); each with a quote, needs and channels.

## Slide 4 — Positioning map (axes with double arrows, bubbles sized by share, star for our beer)
Brands: Corsaire Ambrée, Phare Blonde, Abbaye du Moulin, Kerbrew IPA, Lune Rousse, Fjord Pale Ale,
Marée d'Automne (star). Axes: Budget – Premium, Classic – Adventurous.

## Slide 5 — Customer journey (chevrons, freeform emotion curve, smiley and frowny faces)
Stages Discover, Try, Buy, Share, Repeat with touchpoints; low points Buy and Repeat with pain-point
callouts.

## Slide 6 — Marketing mix (snipped tiles)
Product, Price, Place, Promotion, three points each.
